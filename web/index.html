WHAT TO LOOK FOR

Faster motion.
A narrower field.

The solid curve comes from the evolving field. The dashed curve is a stationary kink, centred on the same position. Try 0.9 c: the transition becomes much steeper.

For a free kink, width = √(1 − v²) and E = 8 / √(1 − v²). With a force applied, the field also excites background waves; its total energy includes those waves.

Units are dimensionless, c = 1. Leapfrog: Δx = 0.1, Δt = 0.05. Observation stops at |x| ≥ 75 or t = 120, before boundary effects dominate. Concept art is an imagined setting; the plots are computed measurements.
